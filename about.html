About ToolForge1

ToolForge1 is a home for practical, free online developer tools. Its current collection focuses on common data workflows such as converting CSV and JSON, formatting structured data, and transforming data between formats.

Our approach

  • Simple tools that work in a modern browser.
  • Clear descriptions and direct links to useful utilities.
  • Privacy-aware design, with browser-based processing where supported by the individual tool.
  • Continual maintenance and improvements based on feedback.

The central website is a directory. The tools themselves are hosted separately at CSV & JSON Tools.
