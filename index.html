CSV & JSON Tools — Frequently Asked Questions

Are my CSV and JSON files safe on ToolForge?

The CSV and JSON tools are designed to process the contents you provide directly in your browser, without uploading those contents to a conversion server. This reduces the need to send your data to a third party, but it is not a guarantee of complete privacy: your browser, device, extensions, and page-level analytics may still process information. For safety, do not enter passwords, API keys, or highly sensitive data.

Can I use these CSV/JSON tools offline?

The separate CSV & JSON tools site includes progressive web app (PWA) and offline-support features. Offline availability can depend on whether the site and required assets were loaded and cached beforehand, and some browser or network features may still require a connection. Open the tools site once while online before relying on it offline.

How to compare JSON and view CSV files online?

To compare JSON online, open the JSON Diff & Compare Tool, provide the two JSON documents, and review the differences. To view a CSV online, open the Online CSV Viewer and choose or paste your CSV data. You can also use the CSV to JSON Converter to transform tabular CSV rows into JSON. The tool pages explain their supported inputs and options.
