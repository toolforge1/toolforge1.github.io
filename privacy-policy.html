Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the central ToolForge1 website at toolforge1.github.io. The website is a static information and navigation page that links to the separate CSV & JSON Tools website.

Information you enter

The central page's tool search runs in your browser against a built-in list of tool names. It does not send search terms to a ToolForge1 server. This static page does not provide user accounts or an on-site submission database.

Linked tools and third parties

The linked CSV & JSON tools are a separate website and may have their own analytics, policies, caching behavior, and technical requirements. Review the policies on that website before using it. External sites, including GitHub, are governed by their own privacy policies.

Cookies and analytics

This central page does not intentionally set analytics cookies or run third-party analytics scripts. Your browser, hosting provider, or linked websites may process technical information such as IP address, request time, and device/browser details as part of delivering web pages.

Children and sensitive data

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit confidential information through email or any external service unless you understand how it will be handled.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the site changes. Questions can be sent to csvjsonsupport@gmail.com.
